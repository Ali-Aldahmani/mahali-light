## Third pass, 8 November: everything else

These were the items still listed as open, plus audit findings I hadn't fixed or listed before.

- **A bug that was already there:** a till left on the login page for more than the idle limit (15 minutes) signed the next person out within a second of signing in. Found while clicking through; fixed (`14cb1a8`).
- **A mistake of mine:** what a PO owes after a return left out the VAT on the returned goods. Fixed (`fa3e4c0`).

**Backend trust and money** (`537790c`, migration 058):
- **Overdraft:** taking a bank account below zero needs the new `bank.overdraft` permission (Admin and Manager have it), including from expenses, bills and supplier payments. Every overdraft is logged.
- **Voids:** voiding a collection needs a reason, kept with the record.
- **Reversals:** a void or deleted expense goes back through the account the money used, not today's default.
- **Owner-paid expenses:** they move no shop money and credit owner equity.
- **Retries:** bank account creates and pending adjustment requests don't duplicate.

**Backup and robustness** (`537790c`):
- **Restore:** it holds the backup lock, refuses an unrestorable job up front, and says if uploaded files failed.
- **NAS sign-in:** the username and password fields are gone, because nothing ever used them. The server needs its own access to the share.
- **Bad requests:** a malformed id is a 400, not a 500. Requests with no sign-in aren't written to the error log, which is pruned after 180 days.
- **Reports:** those with no natural limit refuse above 50,000 rows; `sort=margin` works.
- **Stock counts:** a save re-checks the count's status under a lock.
- **Muted notifications:** they stay out of the list and the unread count.

**Known gaps** (`fa3e4c0`):
- **Supplier refunds:** a return marked refunded brings the money into cash or a chosen account, clears Accounts Payable and lowers what the PO shows as paid. It's refused when nothing was overpaid; use a credit note then.
- **Supplier returns:** they can take units from quarantine. `/returns/new` can pick the PO, with the same quantity cap. Cashiers can no longer file them.
- **Serial numbers:** an approved edit adding a line must supply any serial its category requires. The edit form can't add new lines today, so this guards the API.
- **Price check:** one shared check now covers discounts and confirm.
- **Print agent:** by default it answers only pages on this PC or the local network.

**Before you deploy, also:** run migration 058, and tell staff that voids need a reason and that overdrawing needs `bank.overdraft`.

### Gaps closed

- **Bank accounts:** collections, bank refunds and supplier payments choose their account, and the server requires it.
- **Supplier returns:** can be resolved from the supplier page; a supplier return request must name its supplier.
- **Quarantine:** quarantined stock can be released or written off (Inventory › Stock levels › row menu).
- **Warranties:** an exchange gives the replacement unit a warranty; an approved edit voids the warranty of a removed line and covers an added one.
- **Checkout replay:** a replayed checkout with a changed cart updates its draft instead of letting the till confirm the old one.
- **Attendance:** sessions nobody closed are closed on weekends, holidays and at start-up, without invented overtime.
- **Leave:** a double-submitted request can't pass the overlap check twice.
- **Notifications:** they make a sound again (the mp3 files never existed), show a toast (including in Cashier Mode), and the badge uses the server's count.
- **Exports:** Excel shows store time; export files are deleted after download.
- **Hardware agent:** browsers can reach it (CORS and Private Network Access), printer names are complete, temp files are unique.
- **Duplicated tabs:** they no longer get signed out after a token refresh.

## Before you deploy

1. **Run migrations 055, 056 and 057.** 055 adds `customer.void_payment` and `customer.edit_credit_limit` (Admin and Manager gain both); 056 changes what a credit limit of 0 means; 057 adds database rules.
2. **Reload every till.**
3. **Tell staff about these changes:**
   - **Closed drawer:** cash collections, cash expenses, cash bills, cash refunds and exchange differences are refused while the drawer is closed, the same as cash sales. Open the drawer first. Reversals (a void, a deleted expense, a cancelled invoice) can still auto-open it; see the decisions below.
   - **Cashiers:** can't change a credit limit, void a customer payment, or deactivate a customer. They also no longer see bank balances, net position, profit or stock value.
   - **Warehouse:** no longer sees supplier totals, return values, or the PO PDF.
   - **Approvals:** nobody but an Admin can approve their own edit request, return, stock adjustment, stock count, leave or attendance correction. **If a shop has one Manager and no one else, an Admin has to approve that Manager's requests.**
   - **Role changes:** changing someone's role now removes their individual permission overrides.
   - **Deactivating an employee:** now deactivates their login too.
4. **Role permissions:** a restart no longer resets system roles. Until now any change made on the Roles screen was undone at the next restart, so check that each role holds what you expect the first time you run this.
5. **Rehearse on a copy first.** Money reports will change: the P&L now uses its dates, so past figures you noted down from it will differ.

## Decisions for you (first pass; all settled in the second pass above, except FIFO)

These change how money or policy works, so I didn't choose for you.

1. **A credit sale can be refunded in cash while the debt stays.** A 105 invoice paid on credit, returned with the default plan, pays 105 out of the drawer and the customer still owes 105. `validateRefundPlan` only checks the total. Refunds against unpaid or credit tender should go to `credit`, but payments aren't tracked per invoice, so an exact rule needs your call. **This is the most serious open item.**
2. **The drawer approver can be bypassed.** Close with the expected amount, then reopen with the real lower amount: the shortage goes to Owner Equity with no approver. A manual `out` adjustment does the same. Should opening differences and large manual withdrawals need a second approver?
3. **VAT report when an invoice is cancelled later.** Cancelling a September invoice in October lowers Q3's figure after it may have been filed, and Q4 shows no adjustment. Ask your accountant.
4. **Supplier payments ignore returned goods.** A PO of 1,050 with 525 returned still shows 1,050 due, and paying it leaves Accounts Payable in debit. A `refunded` resolution books no money back. Ask your accountant.
5. **Credit limit 0 means unlimited,** and new customers default to 0, so a Cashier can still create a customer and sell on unlimited credit. Should the default be "no credit"?
6. **Reversals can still auto-open a closed drawer** (voiding a collection, deleting an expense, cancelling an invoice, bank-to-cash transfers). I left them, because refusing could block a correction.
7. **Held prices:** a draft keeps its price when confirmed, even if the price or minimum has since changed, and drafts never expire. Is that intended?
8. **Cost 0 through a direct edit** still revalues the stock on hand. Should it need a reason?
9. **Older open items from the hand-off:** replacement items priced ex-VAT against a VAT-inclusive return, no ledger entry for opening stock or no-invoice returns, FIFO (Phase 6) and database rules (Phase 7).

## Found but not fixed

Features or larger changes, listed so they aren't lost:

- **Bank transfer outside the till can't choose an account:** customer collections, refunds and PO payments still use the default account. They need the account picker, plus a bank list those users can read.
- **Supplier returns:** no screen can resolve one, and a supplier return from `/returns/new` records no supplier or PO.
- **Exchanges:** the replacement unit gets no warranty, while the original's is voided.
- **Quarantined stock** has no way out: no release, write-off, or return to supplier.
- **Approved edits on confirmed invoices** don't update warranties or require serials.
- **Checkout replay:** the same `checkoutId` with a different cart returns the first invoice, with no comparison.
- **Retained discount:** leaving out `invoiceDiscount` keeps an old discount on a different cart (direct API call only).
- **Token rotation** can sign out a duplicated browser tab about every 28 minutes.
- **Hardware agent:** from reading the code, browsers can't reach it (no CORS preflight), printer names are cut at the first space, and temp file names can collide. Not run.
- **Attendance sweep:** rows from weekends, holidays or days the server was off may never close, and auto-closed rows can gain false overtime.
- **Leave:** two simultaneous requests for the same dates can both succeed.
- **Bills:** an overdue fixed bill keeps its old amount after an edit.
- **Notifications (`notes.txt`):** Cashier Mode has no bell, `/sounds/notify-*.mp3` don't exist so no alert sound plays, and the badge counts the loaded list rather than `unreadCount`.
- **Smaller:** Excel datetimes are 4 hours behind, report exports stay in `uploads/reports`, reports have no row cap, limits above 100 are cut silently, `/stock/adjustments?status=pending` is fetched several times on page load.
